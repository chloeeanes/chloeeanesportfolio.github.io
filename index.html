An Introduction to Me

A brief backstory and my current endeavors

While working through an illness in my teenage years, I worked hard to keep my grades up, so I could eventually get into college. With hard work, I accomplished this, graduating with a high GPA. Then, I did the same in community college: graduating summa cum laude as a member of the honors societies Phi Theta Kappa and The National Collegiate Chinese Honors Society. Since then, I have gone into remission, earned an academic scholarship soon after transferring to George Mason University, and maintained my 4.0 GPA.

It was not easy to work toward these accomplishments, and because of this, they are perfect examples of my work ethic, and my desire to truly understand the information being taught to me rather than relying on memorization. This certainly will be a quality I carry into my career.

However, academics is not the only area to which I have dedicated my time. In my free time, I love to read manga and webcomics, draw, and play co-op games with friends. Along these lines, recently, I have enrolled in drawing basics courses to improve my skills, so I can eventually create my own webcomics.

Additionally, I spend my time on my decade-old pet care business, which combines my love for animals with gaining experience in running a business. It also gave me a chance to develop soft skills such as maintaining good communication skills, budgeting time, and anticipating clients' needs---including surprise transportation delays and supply shortages for feeding the pets.
